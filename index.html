Universal best practices
• Use bacteriostatic water (0.9% benzyl alcohol) for multi-use vials
• Swirl gently to dissolve — never shake (damages peptide bonds)
• Inject water slowly down the inside wall, not directly onto the powder
• Log reconstitution date; refrigerate at 2–8°C after mixing
• Discard if solution appears cloudy, discolored, or has particles
